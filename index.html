How this is calculated: distance is the great-circle path between the two airports' coordinates. Flight time assumes an average commercial jet cruising speed of ~900 km/h (560 mph), plus a fixed ~30 minute allowance for taxi, takeoff, climb, descent, and landing. Actual flight time varies with aircraft type, route, wind, and air traffic — treat this as a planning estimate, not a booking guarantee.
